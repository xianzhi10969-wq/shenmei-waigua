Use case: illustration-story. Generate ONE original, exquisite hanging chandelier, isolated on genuine transparent background, for the charcoal opening of an illustrated literary art website. No text, room, page design or borders.
REFERENCE ROLES:
Image 1 (illustrated homepage): DRAWING STYLE ONLY. Precise confident fine ink, quiet storybook elegance, the controlled richness of the patterned cloak. Do not copy woman, architecture or symbols.
Image 2 (Met circa1787 chandelier photograph): PRIMARY structural reference ONLY. Learn its slender baluster spindle, open upper canopy, graceful curved candle arms and naturally hanging festoons. Do not copy photograph lighting, room or all its parts.
Image 3 (white Taif glass detail): WHITE MASS AND SMALL FLUTED FLOWER RIMS ONLY. Not a second full chandelier to combine.
DESIGN: A coherent slender eighteenth-century-inspired chandelier, beautifully proportioned, eight candle arms gracefully rising outward from a small central junction, with gently differing heights from slight three-quarter projection. Thin round-section arms, not flat ribbons; long flowing S curves and generous open gaps. Elegant little ivory fluted petal drip cups carrying tall slender candles. A narrow elongated baluster spindle with only a few carefully shaped collars. Small scalloped upper canopy and draped pearl-like chains, each hanging in a believable natural arc between visible attachment points. Pendant lengths vary, with a few larger elongated pear drops and smaller cut beads. One modest elongated bottom finial. NO large central bowl or upside-down dome, NO architectural spire or crown, NO broad strap arms, NO repeated quatrefoil wallpaper inside every part.
Beauty through continuous graceful silhouette, hierarchy and delicate detail, not more ornament. Richness remains in tiny etched lines, ribbing, bead articulation and individual pendant outlines. Keep calm ivory-white surfaces between the fine dark lines. Opaque warm-white candle cups, spindle, thin arms and pendants must remain striking against a dark page; transparent gaps. Do NOT make an all-outline ghost chandelier.
MEDIUM: flat ivory-white and near-black pen illustration. Restrained line variation, crisp contours, carefully placed fine interior lines. No painted shading, gradients, silver highlights, glossy photoreal crystals, reflections, volumetric modeling, gray wash, crosshatched shadow masses, 3D relief or carved stone. Translate the physical design into the drawing language of reference 1.
COMPOSITION: entire chandelier almost fills a 4:5 vertical canvas, short suspension at top, broad open main arms in middle/lower-middle, hanging drops at bottom. Total body width about 82% of height. Thin upper structure, more white concentrated in candle cups and lower arm junctions. Delicacy with strong clear shapes at mobile size. It will later be enlarged/cropped from top and right with editable title crossing the lower-left arms; do not draw that title or a designated rectangular text box. Transparent background everywhere outside the object. No watermark.
