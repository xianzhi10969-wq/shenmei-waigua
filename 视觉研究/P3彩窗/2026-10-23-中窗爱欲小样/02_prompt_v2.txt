=== FOR THE IMAGE MODEL: READ FIRST ===
Purpose: one single stained-glass light design for the "审美外挂 / Atlas of Sensibility" homepage, drawn by the same hand as the approved P1/P2 ink plates. Output ONLY this one lancet panel design. One image, one panel. Not a set, not side-by-side, not a triptych, no other panels.
Goal effect: a quiet, solemn ink cartoon for a stained-glass light, in the spirit of Edward Burne-Jones's Morris & Co. cartoons and Alphonse Mucha's line work — airy, precise, elegant, tender.
(This drawing will later be colour-inverted by us: the ivory paper becomes the black glass and the ink lines become cream lines. So draw it as ordinary black ink line on ivory paper.)

=== SUBJECT & LAYOUT ===
Theme: Eros / longing (爱欲) — love that grows from what is still missing.
Canvas: tall vertical panel. Keep ALL drawing inside the central 55% of the width; the left and right 22% strips stay completely empty paper. The drawing is tall and narrow (about 1 : 2.6). Top 6% and bottom 4% empty.
Subject: ONE single elongated standing female figure, frontal, centred exactly on the vertical axis, in a modest, loose, high-necked classical robe that hides the body shape and falls in long straight vertical folds to her feet, slightly pooling on the ground; a light veil-like mantle drapes from her shoulders (Burne-Jones elongated proportions, about 9 heads tall). Head at about 30% from the top, feet at about 96%. Both arms rise gently above her head; her hands, close together, lift up one open rose on a long stem; the rose is fairly large (about 1.5 times her head) and sits at about 6–14% from the top, centred. Her face is calm, eyes lowered, drawn with very few lines. Hair long and loose, a few flowing strands.
Ground pattern: behind and around her, a SPARSE Morris-style climbing briar-rose vine: two thin stems rising from beside her feet, winding close to her robe in slow symmetrical S-curves up both sides of the figure, ending at shoulder height, with a few small leaves and three or four small five-petal wild roses each side. Lots of empty paper between the vine curls (at least 60% of the background stays empty). The vine never crosses the figure's face or hands.
Symmetry: overall composition near-mirror-symmetric about the vertical axis.

=== DRAWING LANGUAGE (strict) ===
PAPER: flat ivory-white #F6F3ED, perfectly flat; no texture, no vignette.
LINE: crisp, near-vector black ink lines #1E1E1E, continuous and confident, like a Burne-Jones stained-glass cartoon where the outline is the lead line. Two clearly different weights: the whole figure silhouette (robe outline, arms, head, hair outline) and the rose outline are drawn with ONE bold continuous contour (about 4 px at 1024 px); interior drapery folds (few, long, straight), facial features, petals, vine stems and leaves are fine (about 1–1.5 px). Interior fold lines are few and long, never short scribbles. No sketchy strokes, no pencil feel.
SHADING: none. No hatching, no cross-hatching, no stipple, no grey, no gradients, no solid black areas at all. Every area is either empty paper or line.
DETAIL: measured, like a careful botanical/architectural plate; ornament only at a few points.
NEVER: colour, grey wash, watercolour, texture, solid black fills, background scenery, architecture, window frame, border, text, letters, signature, multiple figures, side-by-side panels, 3D, photo, anime.
