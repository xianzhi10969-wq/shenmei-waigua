Gothic Victorian pen-and-ink illustration, clean technical line art on warm cream paper (#F4F1E8). Thin, even, precise black ink outlines (hairline to fine, like a 0.1–0.3mm technical pen), drawn with a ruler-like steadiness; architecture is perfectly symmetrical and frontal or in gentle perspective. Very sparse shading: only fine parallel hatching on the shadow side of forms, never cross-hatching over large areas. Solid black fill used ONLY on one focal element (e.g. a cloak or robe), decorated with small repeating white geometric/star ornaments along its border. At least 60% of the image is empty cream paper. Motifs: pointed gothic arches, slender spires with diamond finials, four-pointed stars, thin railings, polished floor with faint reflection lines, quiet and solemn mood. No color except black ink on cream (optionally a tiny single deep-red accent). NO: grey wash, gradients, watercolor, paper texture, grain, heavy black backgrounds, sketchy or rough lines, thick outlines, comic/anime style, 3D render, photorealism, text.
