Use case: sketch-to-render.
Create ONE finished pen-and-ink illustrated badge of a small antique key for the art exploration feature “好奇心漫游”.
Input 1 is the approved key COMPOSITION sketch on a review sheet. Refine ONLY its large central key. Do not reproduce the sheet, Chinese text, labels or small duplicates.
Inputs 2 and 3 are STYLE references from the same badge family: borrow their poised black ink contours, refined double edges, restrained Art Nouveau ornamental detailing and open ivory space. Do not borrow their objects, waves, windows, ribbons or circular enclosure.

Preserve the approved key's silhouette and proportions: broad organic botanical bow at upper left, three distinct spacious leaf-shaped pierced openings, a short straight narrow shaft pointing diagonally down to the right, two small neck collars, and clearly angular stepped key teeth on the right side near the lower end. Overall axis about 36 degrees clockwise from downward vertical (bow upper-left, teeth lower-right). One connected plausible antique key, generously centered in a square with about 14% margins. Bow about half of key length; no extended handle.

Transform the simple schematic into an exquisite finished miniature drawing. Shape the bow rim as delicate wrought metal with a fine inset parallel contour, graceful tapering leaf ribs between the three open cutouts, subtle incised botanical turns integrated into the bow and neck. Keep the holes large and visually quiet. Give the short shaft a restrained engraved edge, elegant shaped collar joins, and clear believable tooth geometry. Ornament belongs to the key's own metalwork, never outside it. Match the drawing sophistication of references without adding clutter or making it mechanically uniform. Clear primary contours with finer secondary pen lines, very sparing solid-black accents at junctions. Broad areas remain unshaded ivory. Elegant, quiet, curious, inviting.

Palette: charcoal-black ink on an evenly colored very light neutral ivory background (#F6F3ED), same ivory within the key. Flat illustration, no gold, no photorealistic metal, no 3D bevel, no cast shadow, gradients, hatching, paper grain or antique stains. No surrounding frame, circle, ribbon, scroll pedestal, star, eye, animal, insect, flowers, separate foliage, lock, extra keys, text, signature or watermark. Deliver a single complete artwork, not a sketch or generic vector icon.
