- Western blackletter: UnifrakturMaguntia, https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/unifrakturmaguntia, SIL OFL 1.1. Reserved font name observed.

Subsets are renamed to Aura Folio families, preserve original copyright/license metadata, and are self-hosted. License texts are in `dist/assets/licenses`. `scripts/subset-folio-fonts.py` rebuilds them from the original downloaded inputs. Body copy retains readable Song styling; the display font change applies only to titles. No AI-generated lettering is used.
